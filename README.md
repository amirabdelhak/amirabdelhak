## 💫 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="370" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhisheknaiidu/abhisheknaiidu/master/code.gif">

I'm **Amir Abdelhak**, a **Software Engineer at LinkIt** and a **Full-Stack Developer** specializing in the **.NET & Angular** ecosystems, with a strong focus on backend architecture, Clean Architecture, and **Enterprise ERP Solutions**.

I graduated from **Damietta University**, Faculty of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (IT Department, Class of 2024).

💡 **What I Do:**
- 🏢 Contribute to large-scale **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)** systems handling mission-critical business logic.
- 🚀 Architect scalable, production-ready **RESTful APIs** using **ASP.NET Core** and responsive frontends with **Angular**.
- 🛠️ Diagnose, debug, and resolve complex **production bugs** and performance bottlenecks to ensure continuous system availability.
- ⚡ Optimize database performance, complex queries, and data workflows with **SQL Server** and **EF Core**.

🏆 **Highlights:**
- Honored with two prestigious scholarships from the **Ministry of Communications (MCIT)**: **ITI** & **DEPI**.
- Proven experience as a **Team Leader** across multiple end-to-end projects, emphasizing clean code, collaboration, and high delivery standards.
